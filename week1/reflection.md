### Why Relational Databases Matter
This lab showed me the difference between relational databases and spreadsheets. A spreadsheet can work for small tasks. Still, it can get confusing fast when the data grows. Rows and columns do not stay consistent on their own. Duplicate entries can slip in. It is also harder to keep related data together in a clean way.

With a relational database, the system helps keep data correct. Tables connect through keys, so relationships are clearer. It also supports safe access when more than one person works with the data at the same time. From this work, I better understand how MySQL stores structured information and keeps it organized.
